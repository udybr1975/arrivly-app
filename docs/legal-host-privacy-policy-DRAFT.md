## 2. This policy covers hosts, not guests

Bemgu handles two different kinds of personal data, under two different legal roles. The distinction matters, so we set it out plainly.

**Your data — we are the controller.** Your name, email address, property details and billing information are ours to look after. We decide why and how they are processed. This policy describes that.

**Your guests' data — you are the controller and we are the processor.** When you add a guest name to a booking, import a reservation file, or exchange messages with a guest through Bemgu, that information belongs to you. We only handle it on your instructions, to deliver the service you have configured. Our obligations to you for that data are set out in the **Data Processing Agreement**, which forms part of your contract with us.

Guests are told separately how their information is handled, in the **Guest Privacy Notice** linked from every guest page.

## 3. What we collect about you

**Account and identity**
- Name, email address, and an encrypted password (or, if you sign in with Google, a confirmation from Google that you own the address — we never receive your Google password)
- Brand name, logo, accent colour, and optional WhatsApp or phone number

**Your properties**
- Property name, street address, neighbourhood, city, country, floor note, maximum occupancy
- Approximate coordinates, derived from the address
- Photographs you upload
- Wi-Fi details, house rules, check-in instructions and door codes you enter
- Calendar (iCal) links you paste from Airbnb, Booking.com or similar

**Billing**
- Subscription tier, status, trial and renewal dates
- Identifiers issued by our payment provider, Stripe
- **We never see or store your card number.** Card details go directly to Stripe and are never held by Bemgu.

**Technical and usage**
- Server logs generated when you use the site, which may include your IP address, browser type and the pages requested
- Push notification registrations, if you turn on notifications
- Preferences such as which help hints you have dismissed

**Affiliate identifiers**
- If you connect your own Viator, GetYourGuide or Tiqets partner IDs, we store them. These are public identifiers, not secrets.

We do not collect special category data (health, beliefs, biometrics and similar), and you should not enter any into Bemgu.

## 4. Why we use it, and our legal basis

| What we do | Why | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Run your account and guest pages | To deliver what you signed up for | Performance of a contract |
| Take subscription payments | Same | Performance of a contract |
| Send service emails (welcome, trial ending, billing) | To operate your account | Performance of a contract |
| Generate neighbourhood guides and AI content | Core product feature | Performance of a contract |
| Send push notifications | You asked for them | Consent — withdraw any time in your browser or device settings |
| Keep the service secure, prevent abuse and fraud | To protect you, your guests and us | Legitimate interests |
| Keep an internal record of administrative actions | Accountability and security | Legitimate interests |
| Keep accounting records | Required by law | Legal obligation |

Where we rely on legitimate interests, we have weighed our interest against your rights and privacy. You can ask us for the reasoning behind that assessment.

## 5. Administrator access — plainly stated

Bemgu is currently operated by one person. For support and troubleshooting, the administrator can view a host's account as that host sees it.

**Every such access is recorded automatically**, with who did it, which account, and when. That record is kept for 24 months. We consider this a necessary safeguard rather than an optional extra, and we mention it here rather than leaving you to discover it.

## 7. Where your data goes

Our database sits in the EU, our email provider is in the EU, and we have assigned our application servers to Frankfurt.

**We do not claim that all processing happens inside the EU, because it does not.** Google, Stripe, Cloudflare and the push notification services are established outside the EU and receive data there. Those transfers rely on the safeguards described in each provider's terms, including the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and, where certified, the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. The exact mechanism relied on per provider is being confirmed in legal review.

## 8. How long we keep it

**Your account.** We keep your account data for as long as your account is open.

**After you close your account.** We delete your account data, except records we must keep by law — principally accounting records, which we keep for the period Finnish accounting law requires.

**Administrative access records.** 24 months.

**Server logs.** Held briefly by our hosting providers for security and debugging — currently around one day.

**Your guests' data.** Held for the periods set out in the Data Processing Agreement and the Guest Privacy Notice. In summary, guest names and messages are erased 30 days after check-out, and guest notification registrations 7 days after check-out. Booking dates and references remain in your calendar with no person attached to them.

**Backups.** Deleting a record removes it immediately. We do not currently keep a separate backup archive of deleted records.

## 9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you may:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you
- **Correct** anything inaccurate — most of it you can edit yourself in your dashboard
- **Delete** your data, subject to records we must keep by law
- **Restrict** or **object to** processing based on legitimate interests
- **Receive a copy** of the data you gave us, in a portable format
- **Withdraw consent** at any time, where consent is the basis — this does not affect anything done beforehand

To exercise any of these, email **hello@bemgu.app**. We will respond within one month. There is no charge unless a request is clearly unfounded or excessive.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the Finnish supervisory authority:

**Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman** (Tietosuojavaltuutetun toimisto)
Lintulahdenkuja 4, 00530 Helsinki · PO Box 800, 00531 Helsinki
tietosuoja.fi

You may also complain to the authority in your own country of residence.

## 10. Security

We protect your data with encryption in transit, database-level access rules that isolate each host's data from every other host's, server-side enforcement of every permission, and secrets held outside the application code.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach occurs that is likely to put your rights at risk, we will notify you and the supervisory authority as the law requires.

---

## 11. Automated decisions

We do not make decisions about you by automated means that produce legal or similarly significant effects. The AI features generate content — guides, greetings, suggested replies — they do not make decisions about you or your account.

---

## 12. Cookies and local storage

Bemgu uses browser storage strictly to keep you signed in and to remember interface preferences. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not run third-party analytics on your dashboard.
