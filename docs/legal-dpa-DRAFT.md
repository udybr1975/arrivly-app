## 1. What this is and why it exists

When you use Bemgu you put information about your guests into it — a name, some dates, sometimes messages. That information is yours. We only handle it to run the service for you.

European data protection law requires a written agreement whenever one party handles personal data on another's behalf. This is that agreement. It forms part of your contract with us and applies for as long as you have a Bemgu account.

It is written in plain language on purpose. Where it uses a defined term from the GDPR — *controller*, *processor*, *personal data*, *personal data breach* — those terms carry their GDPR meaning.

**Parties**

- **You**, the Host: the person or company holding the Bemgu account.
- **Us**: **U & A Investment and Consultancy Oy** (business ID 3234935-4), Runeberginkatu 17 A 3, 00100 Helsinki, Finland. Contact: hello@bemgu.app

## 2. Who is what

**For your guests' personal data, you are the controller and we are the processor.** You decide what goes in and why. We act on your instructions.

**For your own account data, we are the controller.** That is covered by our Privacy Policy for Hosts, not by this agreement.

**For a narrow set of security data we are the controller.** Server logs and automated anti-abuse checks are run on our own initiative to keep the service working and prevent misuse. We do not process guest personal data for any purpose of our own beyond that.

---

## 3. Your instructions to us

We process guest personal data only on your documented instructions. Your instructions are: this agreement, our Terms of Service, and the settings and actions you take inside the product.

We will not use guest data for our own purposes, will not sell it, will not use it for advertising, and **will not use it to train artificial intelligence models**.

If we believe an instruction from you would breach data protection law, we will tell you and may pause that processing until it is resolved.

If we are ever legally compelled to process guest data in some other way, we will tell you beforehand unless the law forbids us from doing so.

---

## 4. What you are responsible for

You confirm that:

- You have a lawful basis for the guest information you put into Bemgu.
- You have told your guests how their information is used. The Guest Privacy Notice we provide is intended to help you do this, but responsibility for informing your guests remains yours.
- You will not enter special category data — health, religion, political views, biometrics and similar — into Bemgu.
- You will not enter identity document details. Bemgu is not built to hold them.
- Anything you upload, including reservation files exported from a booking platform, you are entitled to upload.

**Bemgu is not your legal compliance record.** Several countries require accommodation providers to register guests with the police or a tourism authority and to keep those records for a set period — for example three years in Spain, and comparable requirements in Italy, Portugal, France and Germany. **Bemgu does not collect the data those rules require, does not submit anything to any authority, and deletes guest names on the schedule in section 8.** Meeting those obligations is yours, using the appropriate national system. Do not rely on Bemgu as that record.

## 5. What we are responsible for

**Confidentiality.** Everyone with access to guest data is bound by confidentiality. Bemgu is currently operated by one person, who is bound accordingly.

**Security.** We maintain the measures in Annex 3, appropriate to the risk.

**Administrator access.** Our administrator can view your account for support and troubleshooting. Every such access is logged automatically with who, what and when, and the log is retained for 24 months. We do not access guest data except to operate, support or secure the service.

**Assistance with guest requests.** If a guest asks you for access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or a copy of their data, we will help you respond, taking into account the nature of the processing and the information available to us. Because guest data sits in your account and much of it you can act on yourself, most requests you can handle directly. Where you cannot, email us and we will act without undue delay.

**Assistance with your wider duties.** We will give you reasonable help with security obligations, breach notification, impact assessments and consultation with a supervisory authority, so far as this relates to Bemgu.

## 7. Sending data outside the EU

Our database, our application servers and our email provider are located in the EU.

**Some sub-processors are not.** Google, Stripe, Cloudflare and the mobile push services are established outside the EU and receive data there. Annex 2 says which. Those transfers rely on the safeguards in each provider's terms, including the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and, where certified, the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. The exact mechanism relied on per provider is being confirmed in legal review.

We do not describe our processing as EU-only, because it is not.

## 8. How long we keep guest data

We apply these periods automatically. They are commitments, not intentions.

| Data | Retention |
|---|---|
| Guest name, surname, email | Erased 30 days after check-out |
| Messages between you and the guest | Erased 30 days after check-out |
| Automatically generated daily suggestions | Erased 30 days after check-out |
| Guest notification registrations | Erased 7 days after check-out |
| Booking dates, reference, source | Retained for your calendar, with no guest identity attached once the above is erased |

Deletion is permanent removal, not archiving.

**When your account ends.** You can export your data for **30 days** after your account closes. After that we delete all guest personal data, except anything we must keep by law. We will confirm deletion in writing if you ask.

---

## 9. If something goes wrong

If we become aware of a personal data breach affecting your guests' data, we will tell you **without undue delay**, and in any case within **48 hours** of becoming aware.

We will tell you what happened, which categories and roughly how many people and records are affected, the likely consequences, and what we are doing about it. If we do not have all of that at first, we will send what we have and follow up.

Notifying your supervisory authority and, where required, your guests is your decision as controller. We will give you what you need to do it.

---

## 10. Checking that we are doing this

On request, and no more than **once a year**, we will provide the information reasonably needed to show we are meeting this agreement.

If that is not enough, you may audit us, or appoint an independent auditor to do so, on 30 days' notice, during business hours, without disrupting the service, and subject to confidentiality. You bear the cost unless the audit finds a material breach by us.

We are a small operation and we would rather answer questions directly than have you commission an audit. Ask us first.

---

## 11. Term, changes and precedence

This agreement runs for as long as you have a Bemgu account and any period afterwards in which we still hold guest data.

We may update it to reflect changes in law or in the service. For anything that materially reduces your protections we will give you **30 days'** notice, and you may terminate if you object.

If this agreement conflicts with our Terms of Service on the handling of guest personal data, **this agreement wins**.

# Annex 1 — What is processed

**Subject matter.** Providing the Bemgu guest concierge service.

**Duration.** For as long as you have an account, plus the retention periods in section 8.

**Nature and purpose.** Storing and displaying stay information to your guests; enabling messages between you and them; generating local recommendations and greetings; sending notifications you or your guest have enabled.

**Categories of data subject.** Guests staying at properties you manage.

**Types of personal data.**
- First name; sometimes surname and email address
- Check-in and check-out dates, booking reference and source
- Messages exchanged between guest and host
- Questions typed into the chat assistant
- Device notification registrations
- IP address, in server logs and anti-abuse checks

**Not processed.** Identity documents, passport or ID numbers, date of birth, nationality, home address, payment card details, special category data.

**How it arrives.** Entered by you; imported from a reservation file you upload; synchronised from a calendar link you provide (dates only — calendar feeds carry no guest names); or provided by the guest through the guest page.

# Annex 3 — Security measures

- Encryption in transit for all connections
- Encryption at rest for the database, as provided by our hosting provider
- Database-level access rules isolating each host's data from every other host's, enforced by the database rather than by application code
- Server-side enforcement of every permission; guest access is granted only by a booking token or a per-property key
- Private information — door codes, entry instructions, Wi-Fi credentials — released only to a guest whose booking token has been verified server-side for the current dates
- Secrets held in the hosting environment, never in application code; source code contains no credentials
- Administrator actions logged automatically and retained 24 months
- Automated deletion of guest personal data on the schedule in section 8
- Application servers assigned to the EU region
