## 2. What Bemgu is

Bemgu gives each of your properties a personalised guest page, delivered by a QR code or
a link: Wi-Fi details, check-in information, house rules, a neighbourhood guide, local
events, your own recommendations, bookable experiences, and an AI concierge. You manage
it all from a host dashboard: properties, bookings, calendars, branding, sharing and
billing.

Some of what appears on your guest pages is **generated by artificial intelligence** —
the neighbourhood guide, city events, greetings, chat replies. AI content can be wrong,
out of date or incomplete. It is clearly labelled as AI on the page. You are responsible
for reviewing what your guests see, and anything safety-critical — door codes, gas,
electricity, emergency instructions — must come from you, not from the AI.

We improve Bemgu continuously. Features may be added, changed or removed. If we remove
something significant that you are paying for, section 12 applies.

## 4. Free trial

Every new account starts with a **14-day free trial**. You choose a plan and add a card
at checkout, but **nothing is charged during the trial**. When the trial ends, your
subscription starts and the first monthly payment is taken, unless you cancel first —
cancelling during the trial costs nothing.

If your trial ends without an active subscription, your guest pages stop being shown to
guests. Your data is not deleted; it is kept as described in the Privacy Policy, so you
can subscribe later and continue where you left off.

We also offer a separate 48-hour demo sandbox that needs no card. Demo accounts and
their data expire and are removed automatically.

Except where the law requires otherwise, payments already made are not refunded,

## 9. Third-party services

Your guest pages can show experiences and tickets from third-party marketplaces
(currently Viator, GetYourGuide and Tiqets). Bookings made there are contracts between
the guest and that marketplace — we are not a party to them. Links to these marketplaces

GetYourGuide and Tiqets partner accounts, in which case those providers pay commissions

identifiers, which is stated in your dashboard.

Calendar connections (for example an Airbnb iCal link) rely on the format and
availability of the other platform's feed, which we do not control.
